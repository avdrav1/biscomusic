          About the data

          Songs, debuts, play counts and setlists come from the Biscuits Internet Project, the fan-run database of every show.

          Recordings stream from the Disco Biscuits collection on archive.org, shared by the tapers who recorded the shows. Where a debut wasn't taped, you hear the earliest recording that exists.

          How versions are ranked. Top rated versions combine BIP listeners' star ratings of each performance with archive.org reviews of the whole night's recordings. Each site's ratings are compared against that site's own average, more votes count more, and archive.org counts half because it rates the whole show and the tape, not just the song.
